contributing

if want to add more information to the wiki, or you notice anything wrong, don't hesitate to contribute.

writing/editing articles

articles on the wiki are written in Markdown format, which you can learn how to use in 20 minutes, check out the cheat sheet.

you can see an example of one of our wiki article in Markdown format here: example

make sure to include sources when writing articles, preferrably primary sources.

submitting

for the technically inclined, you can simply send a pull-request on Github: github.com/neontomo/block-ai-wiki

new articles are added to the /md folder.

other ways to contribute

if you are not a technical person, you can still contribute to the wiki by writing/editing articles and sending them directly to my email. I'll fix the formatting for you.

you can also:

  • share the wiki link when people ask about blocking AI
  • send me suggestions for articles you want covered
  • bookmark the wiki - more is coming soon!
