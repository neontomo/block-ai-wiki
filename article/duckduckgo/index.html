disable AI content

method 1

  • add noai. in the URL: noai.duckduckgo.com

to make this step permanent, change your search engine in the browser to point to:

  • https://noai.duckduckgo.com/?q=%s

method 2

  • go to Settings#aifeatures

  • disable the settings you are uninterested in

note: this settings page does not show up if you are on the noai.duckduckgo.com version, as it already strips out AI.
