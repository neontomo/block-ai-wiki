# Facebook

unfortunately, Meta does not allow removing the blue circle Meta AI icon. you have limited options to retake control, but you might still want to take action on those options.

## opt-out of sharing data for training AI models

opting out is not automatically fullfiled when submitting the form. depending on the country you live in, Facebook will respond to your request differently. for example, in the US there are fewer data privacy laws to protect the user.

in general, Meta will train AI models on any data that is publicly available on the internet.

### step 1

1. go to the [Data subject rights for third-party information used for AI at Meta](https://www.facebook.com/help/contact/510058597920541) form

2. read the full form description to understand what you're agreeing to

3. toggle the `I want to object to or restrict the processing of my personal information from third parties used for developing and improving AI at Meta` checkbox at the bottom of the form

4. fill in `Country of residence`, `First name`, `Surname`, `Email address`, etc.

5. submit the form by clicking `Send`

### step 2

this method may work in certain regions, but don't count on it.

1. set your profile to Private

### step 3 (EU only)

1. open `Privacy Center` in your settings

2. go to `Data settings`

3. go to `Off-Facebook activity`

4. go to `Manage your data`

5. turn off `Data sharing` and `A.I. model training`

### step 4

1. object to using your data for AI training by vising [the objection form](https://www.facebook.com/help/contact/712876720715583)

--

## disabling Meta AI Comment Summaries

1. click your profile picture and go to `Settings & privacy` > `Settings`

2. go to `Audience & visibility`

3. go to `Posts`

4. toggle `Show comment summaries on posts` to `off`

--

## turn off Meta AI in Chat

Meta AI in Chat cannot be completely disabled, but you can mute it.

1. tap the blue circle

2. tap the blue arrow in the upper-right corner

3. tap the `ⓘ` icon in the upper-right corner

4. press `Mute` and select `Until I change it`
