# LinkedIn

LinkedIn does not give users many options to disable AI features, but rather leaves AI features always on but mostly optional to use. for example, to not use AI writing tools, simply do not press the AI button for it.

--

## disable AI-powered job search

LinkedIn is gradually retiring classic job search starting in September 2026 and replacing it with the AI-powered job search. for now, the classic search can still be accessed by visiting the old link directly.

1. go to [classic job search](https://www.linkedin.com/jobs/search)

**note:** if the link fails, try using a computer and make sure you are signed in

--

## opt-out of sharing data for training AI models

1. go to [Data Privacy settings](https://www.linkedin.com/mypreferences/d/categories/privacy)

2. go to `Data for Generative AI Improvement`

3. toggle `Use my data for training content creation AI models` to `off`

--

## disable Harmful message detection with AI

1. go to [Data Privacy settings](https://www.linkedin.com/mypreferences/d/categories/privacy)

2. go to `Messaging experience` > `Harmful message detection`

3. toggle `Harmful message detection` to `off`

--

## block AI content

LinkedIn these days is over-run by AI content. I find that blocking this content is error-prone, no matter what script you use. instead, I suggest to use a highlighter to indicate that content is probably generated so that you can make an informed choice of whether to ignore the post or not.
