# Android

unfortunately, none of these steps will ensure that your device is clean from AI, but it will dramatically limit how much you see it in your day-to-day.

--

## disable Digital Assistant

1. go to `Settings`

2. go to `Apps`

3. go to `Default apps`

4. go to `Digital assistant app`

5. select `None`

--

## disable Gemini

1. go to `Settings`

2. go to `Apps`

3. click the the dots in the top right corner

4. click `Show system`

5. click the search button, type in `Gemini` and open it

6. click `Disable`

--

## remove Digital Assistant gesture

if your phone has a gesture that activates the assistant, try these steps. bear in mind that your phone may have slightly different options.

1. go to `Settings`

2. go to `Gestures`

3. go to `Press and hold power button`

4. replace `Digital assistant` with `Power menu`

--

## disable AI Circle Search

bear in mind that your phone may have slightly different options.

1. go to `Settings`

2. go to `Intelligent assistance`

3. go to `AI Circle Search`

4. toggle `AI Circle Search` to `off`
