# DuckDuckGo

--

## disable AI content

**note**: these methods may stop working at any time

### method 1

- add `noai.` in the URL: [noai.duckduckgo.com](https://noai.duckduckgo.com/)

to make this step permanent, change your search engine in the browser to point to:

- `https://noai.duckduckgo.com/?q=%s`

### method 2

- go to [Settings#aifeatures](https://duckduckgo.com/settings#aifeatures)

- disable the settings you are uninterested in

**note:** this settings page does not show up if you are on the [noai.duckduckgo.com](https://noai.duckduckgo.com/) version, as it already strips out AI.
