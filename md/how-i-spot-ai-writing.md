# how I spot AI writing

here are some things to look for if you want to become better at spotting AI-writing, don't want your own writing to be mistaken for an AI, or if you're looking to write your own scripts to limit AI content on the internet.

if you figure out more, please let me know.

--

## examples

### 1. synonym rotation

`the user`

`the customer`

`the client`

are three ways to say similar things. most of the time, using them interchangeably makes no difference to the end reader, but in other situations it can be crucial to use the correct phrase. the AI loves rotating synonyms, even when it reduces the quality of the sentence.

### 2. hedging

`it's important to note that this may potentially help ...`

`no single approach fits all`

`you might consider`

the AI shirks ownership by making vague, general statements that can never be traced back to itself when the response leads to a failure.

### 3. passive voice & overly verbose verbs

`the committee approved the budget` -> `the committee did the approving`

`she analyzed it` -> `it was analyzed by her` / `she performed an analysis`

`examine` -> `perform an examination of`

the AI loves to use passive voice and to make verbs longer without it adding additional meaning or value.

### 4. marketing adjectives

`seamless`

`robust`

`cutting-edge`

`transformative`

lots of *synergy* here.

### 5. run-ons

for example, four ideas stitched into one sentence.

### 6. phrasal verbs

`start` -> `spin up`

`message` -> `reach out`

`begin` -> `dive into`

AIs love to turn concepts that have clear verbs into something more metaphorical.

### 7. em-dashes and n-dashes
`—` and `–`

### 8. words that humans rarely use
`delve`

`pivotal`

`embark`

`spearheaded`

### 9. comparisons
`it's not ..., it's about ...`

`but it's less about ... and more about ...`

### 10. creepy flattery
`what you said about ... really resonates`

`incredible. that is exactly the kind of ... that ...`

### 11. dramatics + hyperbole
`let's keep building and protecting`

`I learned the hard way`

`something shifted for me recently`

### 12. unusual gratefulness + helpfulness
`appreciate your voice here`

`thanks for the thoughtful response`

`thanks for your question`

`wishing you clarity and strength on your own path`

### 13. unusual summarisations + breakdowns
`to summarize ...`

`in conclusion ...`

`the key takeaway is ...`

`if we break this down ...`
